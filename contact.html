Before you write

Do you accept guest articles or pitches?
Yes — we welcome pitches from architects, interior designers, and writers. Please send a short outline (150–200 words) and a link to your portfolio. We respond within two weeks.
Can I republish your content?
Our articles are for inspiration and education. You may quote or reference with proper attribution and a link back to the original. For full republication or commercial use, please contact us for licensing.
Do you offer design consultations?
We are a editorial publication, not a design firm. However, our editors occasionally take on speaking engagements and workshops. Reach out with details.
I’m having trouble with the newsletter. Who can help?
Email hello@handmadespacestories.com with the subject “Newsletter help” and we’ll sort it out.
