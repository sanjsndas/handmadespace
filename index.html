Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Handmade Space Stories different from other design blogs?
We publish long-form, original reporting and practical guides written by practicing architects and designers. No listicles, no fluff — just deep dives into materials, space planning, and the principles behind good design.
Do you accept guest articles or pitches?
Yes, we welcome pitches from architects, interior designers, and writers. Please send a short outline and your portfolio to editors@handmadespacestories.com. We respond within two weeks.
How can I use your articles for my own project?
Our content is for inspiration and education. You’re welcome to reference our articles with proper attribution and a link back to the original. For commercial use, please contact us for licensing.
Do you have a print or digital issue?
Not yet. We’re a fully digital publication, but we’re exploring a quarterly print edition. Subscribe to our newsletter to be the first to know.
