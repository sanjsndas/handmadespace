About Handmade Space Stories

An independent editorial publication exploring the intersection of craft, sustainability, and everyday living — one space at a time.

Our Story

Handmade Space Stories began in 2019 as a personal notebook — a place to document the quiet details that make a home feel lived-in and intentional. What started as a series of sketches and material samples shared among a small circle of architects and designers has grown into a full editorial journal read by thousands of homeowners, design professionals, and curious minds around the world.

We believe that good design is not about trends or perfection. It is about understanding how materials age, how light moves through a room, and how a space can support the rhythms of daily life. Every article we publish is an invitation to look closer — at the grain of a timber floor, the patina of an unlacquered brass tap, or the way a courtyard brings sky into the center of a home.

What We Publish

Our editorial covers six core areas, each explored through long-form articles, practical guides, and case studies:

  • Interior Design — the art of arranging rooms, textures, and objects to create atmosphere and function.
  • Architecture — residential projects, from courtyard houses to passive-energy envelopes.
  • Home Design — practical advice on kitchens, living spaces, and the details that make a house work.
  • Materials — how stone, timber, metal, and concrete behave over time, and why patina matters.
  • Lighting — daylight strategy, layered artificial light, and the science of visual comfort.
  • Space Planning — how to zone, divide, and organize homes for real life, not just photographs.

We do not publish listicles, sponsored content, or recycled press releases. Every piece is written by a practicing architect, designer, or journalist with direct experience in the field.

Our Editorial Principles

“A home is not a showroom. It is a living, breathing space that changes with the people inside it. Our job is to help you understand the principles — so you can make your own choices with confidence.”

— Elena Marchetti, Editor-in-Chief

We hold to three simple principles:

  • Depth over speed. We take the time to research, interview, and test ideas before publishing.
  • Honesty over hype. We recommend materials and methods we have used ourselves, and we tell you when something is not worth the investment.
  • Craft over trend. We are more interested in how a surface will look in ten years than in what is fashionable this season.
