Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information at Handmade Space Stories.

Last updated: 15 March 2025

Handmade Space Stories (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the website handmadespacestories.com (the “Site”). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Site and the choices you have associated with that data.

In short: We collect only what we need to respond to you, improve our editorial content, and send you the newsletter if you ask for it. We never sell your data. You can request deletion at any time by writing to privacy@handmadespacestories.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our service to you.

Personal Data

While using our Site, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you (“Personal Data”). Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  • Email address (when you subscribe to our newsletter or use the contact form)
  • First name and last name (when you contact us or sign up)
  • Cookies and Usage Data (see our Cookies Policy)

Usage Data

We may also collect information on how the Site is accessed and used (“Usage Data”). This Usage Data may include information such as your computer’s Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), browser type, browser version, the pages of our Site that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages, unique device identifiers and other diagnostic data.

2. How We Use Your Data

Handmade Space Stories uses the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Site
  • To notify you about changes to our Site
  • To allow you to participate in interactive features of our Site when you choose to do so
  • To provide customer care and support (e.g., responding to your contact form submissions)
  • To gather analysis or valuable information so that we can improve our Site and editorial content
  • To monitor the usage of our Site
  • To detect, prevent and address technical issues
  • To send you our newsletter, if you have opted in

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are from the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for collecting and using the personal information described in this Privacy Policy depends on the Personal Data we collect and the specific context in which we collect it.

  • Consent: You have given us permission to do so (e.g., newsletter subscription).
  • Contract: The processing is necessary for a contract with you (e.g., responding to your inquiry).
  • Legitimate Interests: The processing is in our legitimate interests and not overridden by your rights (e.g., improving our Site).
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with the law.

4. Data Retention

We will retain your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use your Personal Data to the extent necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies.

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we retain your email address until you unsubscribe. Contact form submissions are kept for up to 24 months for reference, unless you request earlier deletion.

5. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Your Data Protection Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights. Handmade Space Stories aims to take reasonable steps to allow you to correct, amend, delete, or limit the use of your Personal Data.

You have the following rights:

  • The right to access, update or delete the information we have on you. You can contact us to request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it.
  • The right of rectification. You have the right to have your information corrected if it is inaccurate.
  • The right to object. You have the right to object to our processing of your Personal Data.
  • The right of restriction. You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal information.
  • The right to data portability. You have the right to be provided with a copy of the information we have on you in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • The right to withdraw consent. You also have the right to withdraw your consent at any time where we relied on your consent to process your personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@handmadespacestories.com.

7. Service Providers

We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our Site (“Service Providers”), to provide the Site on our behalf, to perform Site-related services, or to assist us in analyzing how our Site is used. These third parties have access to your Personal Data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

Currently, we use the following categories of service providers:

  • Email newsletter platform (for delivery of our newsletter)
  • Analytics providers (to understand site usage)
  • Hosting and infrastructure services

8. Links to Other Sites

Our Site may contain links to other sites that are not operated by us. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that third party’s site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit.

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our Site does not address anyone under the age of 16 (“Children”). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 16. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us. If we become aware that we have collected Personal Data from children without verification of parental consent, we take steps to remove that information from our servers.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the “Last updated” date at the top.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • By email: privacy@handmadespacestories.com
  • By mail: Handmade Space Stories, Via dei Piatti 8, 20123 Milano, Italy
